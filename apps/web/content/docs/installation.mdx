FlowReco Desktop is available for macOS and Windows. This guide walks you through downloading, installing, and granting the necessary permissions so you can start recording right away.

## Download

Use the local [download page](/download) or the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Atharvsinh-codez/FlowReco/releases) to find signed installers once they are published. Until then, follow the repository build instructions.

## macOS

### System Requirements

- **macOS 13 (Ventura) or later**
- Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) or Intel processor
- At least 4 GB of RAM
- 200 MB of available disk space

### Installation Steps

1. Download the `.dmg` file from the [available releases](https://github.com/Atharvsinh-codez/FlowReco/releases), or build it from source.
2. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file.
3. Drag the **FlowReco** icon into the **Applications** folder.
4. Open FlowReco from your Applications folder or Spotlight search.
5. If macOS shows a warning that FlowReco was downloaded from the internet, click **Open** to proceed.

### Granting Permissions

FlowReco needs a few system permissions to record your screen, camera, and microphone. macOS will prompt you for these when you first try to use each feature.

**Screen Recording**

1. When you first start a recording, macOS will ask you to grant Screen Recording permission.
2. Click **Open System Settings** when prompted.
3. Navigate to **Privacy & Security** > **Screen Recording**.
4. Toggle the switch next to **FlowReco** to enable it.
5. You may need to restart FlowReco for the change to take effect.

**Microphone**

1. When you first enable microphone recording, macOS will prompt for Microphone access.
2. Click **Allow** in the dialog.
3. You can manage this later in **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Microphone**.

**Camera**

1. When you first enable the camera overlay, macOS will prompt for Camera access.
2. Click **Allow** in the dialog.
3. You can manage this later in **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Camera**.

**Accessibility (Optional)**

Some features, like global keyboard shortcuts, may require Accessibility access. If prompted:

1. Open **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Accessibility**.
2. Click the **+** button and add FlowReco from your Applications folder.

## Windows

### System Requirements

- **Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) or Windows 11**
- 64-bit processor
- At least 4 GB of RAM
- 200 MB of available disk space

### Installation Steps

1. Download the `.exe` installer from the [available releases](https://github.com/Atharvsinh-codez/FlowReco/releases), or build it from source.
2. Run the downloaded installer.
3. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the installation.
4. FlowReco will be added to your Start menu and optionally to your desktop.
5. Launch FlowReco from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.

### Granting Permissions

**Microphone and Camera**

1. Windows will prompt you to allow microphone and camera access when you first use these features.
2. Click **Yes** or **Allow** when prompted.
3. You can manage these permissions in **Settings** > **Privacy** > **Microphone** and **Camera**.

**Screen Recording**

Windows does not require a separate screen recording permission. FlowReco uses native Windows APIs to capture your screen without additional setup.

## Auto-Updates

FlowReco Desktop automatically checks for updates and will notify you when a new version is available. When an update is ready:

1. You will see a notification in the FlowReco app.
2. Click to download and install the update.
3. FlowReco will restart with the latest version.

Updates include new features, performance improvements, and bug fixes. We recommend keeping FlowReco up to date to get the best experience.

## Verifying Your Installation

After installing FlowReco, verify everything is working:

1. Open FlowReco Desktop.
2. Sign in with your FlowReco account or create a new one.
3. Click the record button and select a screen or window.
4. Record a few seconds and stop the recording.
5. If the recording plays back successfully, you are all set.

If you run into issues, check the permissions sections above or visit the [FlowReco community Discord](https://discord.gg/y8gdQ3WRN3) for help.

## Uninstalling

**macOS**: Drag FlowReco from the Applications folder to the Trash. To remove preferences and data, also delete `~/Library/Application Support/so.cap.desktop`.

**Windows**: Open **Settings** > **Apps** > **Installed Apps**, find FlowReco, and click **Uninstall**. Follow the prompts to remove it.
