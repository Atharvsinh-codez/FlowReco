FlowReco is the open source alternative to Loom. It is a lightweight, powerful screen recording tool that lets you capture your screen, camera, and microphone, then instantly share a link with anyone. No upload wait times, no file size limits, no friction.

Whether you are walking a teammate through a bug, recording a product demo, creating a tutorial, or sending async updates, FlowReco makes it fast and effortless.

## Why FlowReco?

**Open source and transparent.** FlowReco is built in the open with a permissive license. You can inspect every line of code, contribute features, or self-host on your own infrastructure.

**Instant sharing.** Record and get a shareable link in seconds. No waiting for uploads to finish or files to process.

**Professional editing built in.** Studio Mode gives you a full editing suite with background customization, zoom effects, trimming, and more, right inside the app.

**AI-powered captions.** FlowReco automatically generates accurate captions for every recording, making your content accessible and searchable.

**Cross-platform.** FlowReco Desktop runs natively on macOS and Windows. The web app works in any modern browser for viewing, managing, and sharing your recordings.

**Privacy-first.** Keep projects local or self-host FlowReco on infrastructure you control. The project does not assume a managed FlowReco cloud.

## Key Features

- **Instant Mode** - Click record, capture your screen, and get a shareable link the moment you stop. Perfect for quick walkthroughs and async communication.

- **Studio Mode** - Record locally, then edit with professional tools. Customize backgrounds, add zoom effects, trim footage, and export polished videos.

- **AI Captions** - Automatic transcription and captioning powered by AI. Every recording gets searchable, accessible captions.

- **Camera Overlay** - Include your webcam as a floating overlay to add a personal touch to any recording.

- **Team Sharing** - Share recordings with your team through workspaces. Viewers can leave timestamped comments and emoji reactions.

- **Analytics** - Track who is watching your recordings, how long they watch, and when they engage.

- **Embeds** - Embed your recordings in websites, documentation, and apps with a simple iframe snippet.

- **REST API & Webhooks** - Integrate FlowReco into your workflow programmatically with a full API and real-time webhook notifications.

## Getting Started

The fastest way to get started with FlowReco:

1. **[Install FlowReco Desktop](/docs/installation)** on macOS or Windows
2. **[Record your first FlowReco](/docs/quickstart)** in under a minute
3. **Share the link** with anyone

## Desktop App

FlowReco Desktop targets **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel) and **Windows** (10 and later). Use the local [download page](/download) or the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Atharvsinh-codez/FlowReco/releases) when release artifacts are available.

The desktop app handles recording, camera/microphone capture, and local editing through Studio Mode. Recordings can be shared instantly through the FlowReco cloud or your self-hosted instance.

## Web App

The FlowReco web app is the dashboard and optional sharing service for the server you connect to. It manages published recordings, share settings, analytics, comments, and playback for that deployment.

## Self-Hosting

FlowReco can be fully self-hosted on your own infrastructure using Docker Compose. This gives you complete ownership of your data, recordings, and user accounts. See the [self-hosting guide](/docs/self-hosting) for detailed setup instructions.

## Community and Contributing

FlowReco is open source and community-driven. You can find the source code, report issues, and contribute on [GitHub](https://github.com/Atharvsinh-codez/FlowReco).
